my story

Built from the ground up.

I grew up in St. Louis, Missouri, and went to the University of Alabama for my undergrad and master's in finance. I've always loved adventure and trying new things, so when I got the chance to study abroad on Semester at Sea, I went for it.

Somewhere on that trip I started vlogging and editing clips just for the fun of it. By the time it ended I knew I'd found something that stuck. A lot of my hometown friends haven't had the chance to see the world the way I have, and I want to give them a window into it.

That's what got me into content creation, and I fell in love with it.
